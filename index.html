About me

I grew up in Kuantan and studied graphic design at UiTM Shah Alam. Since then I’ve spent eight years designing for Malaysian businesses, first at an agency in Mont Kiara and now on my own.

My work borrows from the places I know: kopitiam tiles, batik lines, hand-painted signs. I design in English, Bahasa Malaysia and Mandarin.
